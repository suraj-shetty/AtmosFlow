# Fonts

Two families, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their licence text sits
beside them here because the OFL requires it to travel with the files.

| Font | Version | Upstream | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caprasimo | 1.001 | [docrepair-fonts/caprasimo-fonts](https://github.com/docrepair-fonts/caprasimo-fonts) | [OFL-Caprasimo.txt](OFL-Caprasimo.txt) |
| Figtree (Regular, SemiBold) | 2.002 | [erikdkennedy/figtree](https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree) | [OFL-Figtree.txt](OFL-Figtree.txt) |

Neither is mine, and neither is covered by this repository's [LICENSE](../../LICENSE).
The OFL lets you use, study, modify and redistribute these fonts on its own
terms — including inside your own work — provided you do not sell them by
themselves and do not ship them under the reserved names.

Caprasimo sets the display type; Figtree carries everything else. Both are
declared in `pubspec.yaml` and used through `atmos_tokens.dart` rather than
being named directly in widget code.
